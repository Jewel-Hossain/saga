Saga over Kafka

The same order saga, on a log instead of queues. Records are appended to keyed partitions and never removed on consume; each consumer group just moves its own offset. Break a step to see compensations, or break the consumer side to see rebalances, replays and dead-letter topics.

      Kafka terms used here

      Topic · partition
      A topic is split into partitions, each an append-only log. Records get increasing offsets and stay there until retention removes them, whether or not anyone has read them.
      Record key
      The producer hashes the key to pick a partition. Every record here is keyed by order id, so all of one saga's records land in the same partition and are read in order.
      Consumer group · offset
      Each group reads the whole topic independently and stores how far it got as a committed offset. Lag is log end minus committed offset.
      OffsetCommit
      The consumer's equivalent of an ack, but for a position, not a message. Committing after processing gives at-least-once delivery.
      Rebalance
      When a consumer stops heartbeating for session.timeout.ms, the group coordinator reassigns its partitions. The new owner starts at the last committed offset and rereads anything processed but not committed.
      Idempotent producer
      enable.idempotence=true gives the producer an id and per-partition sequence numbers, so the broker drops a retried batch it has already written. With it off, a lost ack means a duplicate record.
      Dead letter topic (DLT)
      Kafka has no nack or DLX. A consumer that can't process a record writes it to a .DLT topic itself and commits past it, otherwise the whole partition is stuck behind it.
      No broker-side routing
      Unlike a RabbitMQ exchange, Kafka doesn't filter. In choreography every group reads every event on saga.events and ignores the types it doesn't care about.
      Replay
      Because records stay in the log, a new or reset consumer group can rebuild state from offset 0. That's how saga-audit reconstructs saga status without a saga table.
